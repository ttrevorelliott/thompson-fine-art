About Us

A Boutique Firm Built on Trust & Expertise

Thompson & Martinez Fine Art Appraisals has served collectors, attorneys, and institutions throughout San Diego since 1999. Based in La Jolla, we bring rigorous scholarship, professional integrity, and personal attention to every engagement.

Our appraisers hold active membership in the American Society of Appraisers (ASA) and adhere strictly to the Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice (USPAP), ensuring every report we produce is defensible, credible, and accepted by insurance companies, the IRS, and courts of law.

USPAP (Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice) is the ethical and performance standard for the appraisal profession. Compliance is required for IRS submissions and is the recognized benchmark for courts, lenders, and insurers. All our appraisers maintain current USPAP certification.

We recommend updating insurance appraisals every 3–5 years or following any significant change in the art market. IRS-purpose appraisals must be completed no earlier than 60 days before the contribution date and no later than the tax return due date.
